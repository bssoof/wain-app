During smoke, the consumer discovery flow exposed that QA venues were not discoverable. Root cause was seed schema drift:

- `operational_status` was seeded as `open`, while app discovery filters require `active`.
- `tags` was missing, but `Venue.fromJson` requires it when venues are parsed.
- `city_key` was missing, reducing deterministic city lookup coverage.

The seed now writes discoverable venue fixtures with `operational_status=active`, `city_key=ramallah`, and complete `tags`/`all_tags`.
